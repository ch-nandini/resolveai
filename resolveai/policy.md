# Refund Policy (ResolveAI demo store)

## §1 Eligibility and identity
A refund request must come from the customer who placed the order. If the order cannot be found, or it belongs to a different account, do not issue a refund; route the case to a specialist for identity review.

## §2 Not delivered, lost, or missing package
If tracking shows the package as lost, returned to sender, or still in transit more than 5 days after the promised delivery date, issue a full refund. If tracking shows delivered without signature confirmation and the customer reports it never arrived, issue a full refund. If tracking shows delivered with signature confirmation, open a carrier investigation and route to a human. If the package is still in transit and within 5 days of the promised date, deny for now and ask the customer to wait.

## §3 Damaged or defective items
Items that arrive broken, cracked, damaged, or defective are eligible for a full refund within 60 days of delivery. This applies to final-sale items too. Requests more than 60 days after delivery are denied.

## §4 Wrong item received
If the customer received the wrong item, size, or color, issue a full refund within 60 days of delivery. Requests more than 60 days after delivery are denied.

## §5 Late delivery
If an order was delivered more than 2 days after the promised date, issue a goodwill credit of 15% of the order total, capped at $25. If it arrived on time (within 2 days of the promised date), deny.

## §6 Change of mind
Customers who no longer want an item may get a full refund within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is returned. Final-sale items are not eligible for change-of-mind refunds. Requests more than 30 days after delivery are denied.

## §7 Duplicate requests
Orders that have already been refunded are not eligible for another refund. Deny duplicate requests.

## §8 Automation limits and risk review
The automated agent may not issue refunds over $200, refunds where the fraud score is 0.70 or higher, refunds for customers with 3 or more refunds in the last 30 days, or refunds over $50 for accounts younger than 14 days. These cases are routed to a human operator for approval.
